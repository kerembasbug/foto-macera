📸 Photo Quest — A Camera Game for Kids

Photo Quest is a colorful camera simulator that teaches the basics of photography through play. Explore aperture, shutter speed, ISO, zoom and white balance through fun missions, and earn stars for every great shot!

🎮 HOW TO PLAY
Every level has a mission: melt the background behind your friend's portrait, freeze a flying butterfly, or take a bright photo of a cat in the city at night. Move the sliders, see the result instantly, then press "SNAP!". Your photo develops and your stars light up one by one!

🌍 FIVE WORLDS, 15 MISSIONS
🔵 Aperture World — Blurry backgrounds and depth
⏱️ Speed World — Freeze motion or leave a trail
✨ Light World — Clean shots in low light
🔭 Zoom World — Zoom in, zoom out, no shaking
🎨 Color World — Catch the color of light

⭐ BUILT FOR LEARNING
• Works like a real camera
• Click, the friendly camera mascot, guides every step
• A tip on every mission and three-star scoring
• Pro mode: win the crown without tips or the light meter
• Daily Mission and daily streak
• Free Mode: shoot your own photos in 12 scenes and save them to your album
• English and Turkish

👪 PARENTS' CORNER
See which concepts your child is learning and how they are progressing. Protected by a parental gate.

👶 SAFE FOR KIDS
• NO ads
• NO in-app purchases
• No account or personal information required, no data collected

Take your first step into the magical world of photography with Photo Quest. Play, learn and become a little photo pro! 📷✨
```
